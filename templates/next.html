Before proceeding:

What this checklist leaves to human judgment

Whether an observation is reliable, which action offers the best decision value, whose burdens count, and whether another perspective is independent. A “yes” records your assessment; this page does not verify it. Two unchanged rounds are a proposed pause trigger, not a validated optimum.

Compare feasible actions with waiting or stopping. Record separate costs and uncertainty. Do not turn attention, electricity, money and risk into one score without a defensible basis.

The least expensive useful check

Can we score the decision fairly?

First propose three energy cases with later outcomes withheld. Ask two reviewers whether a consequence-based rubric can distinguish useful choices from persuasive explanations.

If that works, consider a twelve-case pilot comparing an expert, a single model, a structured human–AI group and an unstructured group. Counts are design proposals, not a power calculation.

What must be settled before the pilot

Cases and permissions, information cutoffs, reviewers, resource caps, scoring, minimum useful improvement, harm tolerance, sample rationale, outcome leakage, assignment and stopping rules remain unregistered. No cases have been selected, no participants recruited and no trial run.

A promising energy idea does not establish a process advantage. An imprecise result can remain unresolved. A process that adds overhead without meaningful benefit can lose.

What changed—and what did not

Changed: the process itself is now a hypothesis. The addition defines resource gates, stop conditions and a proposed comparison with credible alternatives.

Unchanged: the original energy model, hypothesis, desk audit, source ledger and framing review. Density must compete fairly. Agreement and confidence remain distinct from evidence; physical entropy remains distinct from other burdens.

Rejected: automatic claims of novelty, multi-AI superiority or a universal information-per-cost score. The directive assumed an existing Merkle research graph; this archival layer was added now.

Still uncertain: whether the protocol earns its overhead and whether decision quality can be scored reliably. The prior-art overlap is clearer; the protocol's performance is not.
