Give your tool the AI reading index, or download the combined reading pack (text). Both are public and require no account. The pack combines the project’s core documents and research records.

Cite and reuse

Use the GitHub repository to inspect history, download the source or propose a change. Original project materials use the MIT license, unless a file states otherwise. You may reuse and modify them, including commercially, while preserving the copyright and permission notices. Third-party sources retain their own licenses.

For scholarly citation, we recommend including the page or record title, its permanent identifier where available, the repository commit and your access date. A commit identifies exactly which version you reviewed. This citation request adds no conditions to the MIT license.
