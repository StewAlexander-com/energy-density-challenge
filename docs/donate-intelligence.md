# Donate Intelligence

A future contribution path, not an active service.

Potential contributions include GPU or CPU time, inference credits, specialized models, simulation resources, laboratory automation, experimental equipment and expertise. At launch, contributors can describe what they can offer in a capability issue. No credits, keys, payments or workloads are collected or executed by the website.

A future system would need reviewed task definitions, isolated execution without default network or device access, contributor consent, resource and spending limits, signed artifacts, reproducible environments, data licenses, provenance, result verification and a stop mechanism. Untrusted research content could never authorize tool execution. Physical laboratory work would remain subject to qualified human institutional review and oversight.

Compute expenditure must also be measured as a research cost. More donated inference is not evidence of progress. Evaluate reproducible useful findings per total resource cost before expanding infrastructure.
